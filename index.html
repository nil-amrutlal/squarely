How it works

Squarely resizes each photo to a 1:1 aspect ratio directly in your browser's memory, using the HTML canvas API. Pad to fit adds a white border around the shorter side so the entire photo stays visible. Crop to fill trims the longer side, centered on the photo, so the square has no borders. Choose whichever suits your batch, then download every converted photo at once as a single .zip file.

Nothing is uploaded to a server — every photo is read, resized, and zipped on your own device, which makes this safe for private photos and fast even for large batches.
